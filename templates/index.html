How the Process Happens

Our advanced AI extracts meaningful clinical data in 3 steps.

1. Upload Image

Upload a standard 12-lead ECG printed on a paper scan. The engine supports images, PDFs, or photos from your camera.

2. Smart Extraction

Our deep learning algorithm automatically separates grid lines from the pure medical signal and reconstructs the data into 1D arrays.

3. AI Diagnosis & Analytics

The system calculates heart rate, SNR, and uses a residual neural network to predict complex cardiovascular pathologies natively.
